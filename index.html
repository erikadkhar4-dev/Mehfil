Our story

A casual Hyderabadi table for everyone

Mehfil is a casual Hyderabadi restaurant known for flavourful biryani and traditional tea with biscuits. From mutton and chicken biryani to chicken 65, kebabs and Chinese favourites, there is something for the whole family.

  • Generous portion sizes
  • Friendly staff and pleasant atmosphere
  • Dedicated family section
  • Dine-in, takeaway and online ordering
